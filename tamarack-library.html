Adding books from shelf photos

  1. Photograph a bookcase in good light, a shelf or two per photo, close enough that spine text is readable. Leaning, angled, and sideways-stacked books are fine as they are; for a tall stack, an extra close-up helps.
  2. Send the photos to Claude in chat and say which room and bookcase it is. Claude counts the shelves (1 = top) and each book's place from the left, reads the spines, and sends back a tamarack-library-import.json file. Books in a sideways stack share one spot, lettered top to bottom (5a, 5b, 5c…).
  3. Load that file below, check the preview, and import. Books Claude wasn't sure about come in flagged Needs review.
  4. Covers, ISBNs, and years fill in automatically in the background after import. Open any book to pick a different cover.

Data & Sync

Your catalog saves on this device as you go and syncs to Dropbox so it's the same on your phone and desktop.

Dropbox

Checking…

The library has its own Dropbox connection, separate from your other apps. The catalog lives in Dropbox/Railey Library/tamarack-library.json. Connect once on each phone or computer. Changes from two devices are merged book by book, so editing on your phone and desktop never wipes out the other's changes.

At a glance

Covers & details

Covers are found automatically in the background from Open Library and Google Books whenever new books come in (only when the title and author clearly match). It also fills in blank ISBN, year, publisher, page count, and keywords, and never overwrites what you've typed. For a wrong or missing cover, open the book and tap “Look up cover & details” to pick one.

Backup & export

Merging a backup adds any books it has that you don't, and keeps whichever version of each book was edited most recently. It never deletes anything.
